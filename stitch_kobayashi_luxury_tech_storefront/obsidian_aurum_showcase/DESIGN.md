## Brand & Style

This design system expresses quiet authority, exquisite craftsmanship, and technological prestige. Tailored for discerning connoisseurs of state-of-the-art consumer hardware, horology, and artisanal perfumery, the aesthetic balances the timeless elegance of classic luxury editorial with the precision of cutting-edge industrial design.

The visual ethos blends **Dark Luxury Minimalism** with **Atmospheric Layering**:
- Deep midnight navy foundations anchor the canvas, shifting focus onto ultra-high-resolution product imagery and metallic accents.
- Warm champagne gold serves as the sole emissive light source, used selectively to convey rarity and precision.
- Restrained editorial pacing uses deliberate negative space and disciplined vertical rhythm, eschewing unnecessary ornamentation for architectural clarity.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immersive nocturnal atmosphere, built around midnight deep-sea values and calibrated gold accents.

### Palette Architecture
- **Canvas Primary (`#06101f`)**: The foundational deep obsidian-navy base, creating boundless depth.
- **Surface Secondary (`#0b1a30`)**: Structural framing, section division, and navigational backdrops.
- **Card Surface (`#101f3a`)**: Default resting state for product cards, media modules, and content clusters.
- **Elevated Surface (`#18294a`)**: Modals, flyouts, floating overlays, and active/hover states.
- **Primary Accent (`#d4a843`)**: Calibrated warm gold; strictly applied to primary actions, key metric figures, and brand indicators.
- **Light Accent (`#e8c468`)**: Luminous gold highlight used for hover illumination, focus rings, and active states.
- **Primary Text (`#edf0f7`)**: Crisp platinum for titles, high-contrast descriptions, and body elements.
- **Secondary Text (`#7a8599`)**: Muted slate for metadata, secondary captions, and non-active labels.
- **Hairline Border (`rgba(212, 168, 67, 0.10)`)**: Restrained gold hairline applied universally across dividers, cards, and structured borders.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes tension between classical heritage and technical precision:

- **Playfair Display**: Conveys editorial pedigree. Render titles with generous vertical margins. Headings should favor natural casing with tight negative letter tracking on larger scale tokens.
- **DM Sans**: Balances the expressive titles with clean, geometric legibility. Used for all narrative prose, product descriptions, interface menus, and form inputs.
- **Space Mono**: Delivers the tactile feel of laboratory certification and technical instrumentation. Reserved strictly for pricing, mechanical tolerances, component weights, fragrance notes, and dimension data. Always set in uppercase when used for technical labels.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts adhere to an architectural grid centered within a `1200px` maximum viewport boundary.

### Grid Rhythm & Adapting Viewports
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px)**: 12-column layout. Outer container constrained to `1200px` max with `80px` (`margin-desktop`) padding on flanking edges. Gutter fixed at `32px` (`gutter-desktop`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8-column layout. Flanking margin narrows to `40px` (`margin-tablet`), gutters compress to `24px` (`gutter`).
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout. Exterior margins reduce to `20px` (`margin`), gutters remain `16px`. Multi-column product showcases collapse into single vertical stacks or edge-to-edge horizontal carousels with peek overflow.

Product layouts should maintain generous vertical rhythm (`space-2xl` between distinct feature modules) to evoke unhurried museum-like curation.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is defined primarily through layered chromatic density rather than heavy drop shadows, mimicking precision-cut obsidian slabs under subtle directional lighting.

### Surface Hierarchy
1. **Base Floor (`#06101f`)**: Background canvas for full viewports.
2. **Structural Slabs (`#0b1a30`)**: Deep recessed areas such as spec comparison tables and footer bands.
3. **Card Layer (`#101f3a`)**: Primary showcase containers. Elevated by a 1px perimeter stroke of `rgba(212, 168, 67, 0.10)`.
4. **Floating Overlays (`#18294a`)**: Modals, quick-view drawers, and pinned navigation bars. Enhanced with a diffuse ambient luminescence: `0 20px 40px -10px rgba(6, 16, 31, 0.85), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(212, 168, 67, 0.20)`.

### Lighting & Focus Effects
Hovering interactive items does not cast drop shadows; instead, it shifts border visibility from `rgba(212, 168, 67, 0.10)` to `rgba(232, 196, 104, 0.35)` with an inner gold specular feather (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 12px rgba(212, 168, 67, 0.05)`).

## Shapes

The geometric framework balances organic contours and precise industrial corners:

- **Cards & Showcase Modules**: Fixed at `20px` border radius (`rounded-xl` equivalent). This smooth curve softens high-contrast dark surfaces.
- **Buttons, Text Inputs, & Dialog Triggers**: Standardized at `12px` border radius (`rounded-lg` equivalent).
- **Chips, Specification Badges, & Status Indicators**: Standardized at full-pill radius (`9999px`) to cleanly delineate metadata from interactive buttons.
- **Nested Elements**: Inner images or nested containers within a 20px card must scale down to `12px` or `8px` to preserve concentric corner balance.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button**: Solid warm gold (`#d4a843`) background, `#06101f` text, `12px` radius, font weight 600 (`DM Sans`). Height: 48px desktop / 44px mobile. Horizontal padding: `24px`. Hover transforms background to `#e8c468`. Active state compresses scale slightly (0.98).
- **Secondary / Ghost Button**: Transparent background, `1px` border of `rgba(212, 168, 67, 0.30)`, `#edf0f7` text. Hover shifts background to `rgba(212, 168, 67, 0.08)` and border to `#d4a843`.
- **Text Action**: `#d4a843` text in `DM Sans` 500, with a sliding 1px underline on hover.

### Cards & Showcase Panels
- **Structure**: Background `#101f3a`, radius `20px`, outline `1px solid rgba(212, 168, 67, 0.10)`. Internal padding scales from `24px` (mobile) to `32px` (desktop).
- **Hover Behavior**: Card border shifts to `rgba(232, 196, 104, 0.30)` accompanied by a smooth vertical lift of `-4px` using ease `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Technical Badges**: Background `#0b1a30`, border `1px solid rgba(212, 168, 67, 0.15)`, text `#d4a843` rendered in `Space Mono` (`spec-label`). Padding: 4px vertical, 12px horizontal. Full pill radius (`9999px`).
- **Availability / Status Chips**: Subdued `#18294a` pill with a 6px glowing status dot.

### Inputs & Selection Controls
- **Text Inputs**: Height 48px, background `#0b1a30`, text `#edf0f7`, radius `12px`, border `1px solid rgba(212, 168, 67, 0.15)`. Focus applies a `1px solid #d4a843` outline with a subtle `0 0 0 3px rgba(212, 168, 67, 0.15)` glow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Custom 20px squares (or circles), border `1.5px solid rgba(212, 168, 67, 0.30)`. Checked state fills `#d4a843` with `#06101f` icons.

### Specification Tables & Technical Lists
- Alternating subtle rows using `#0b1a30` and `#101f3a`.
- Row borders: Hairline `rgba(212, 168, 67, 0.08)`. Left-hand column labels in slate `#7a8599` (`DM Sans`), right-hand values aligned right in `#d4a843` (`Space Mono`).
